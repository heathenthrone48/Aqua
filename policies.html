Delivery policy

Our estimated delivery time and how orders are fulfilled.

  • NeerBeku aims to deliver water bottles and jars within 2 hours of order confirmation.
  • The 2-hour delivery estimate depends on your location, distance, product availability and order volume.
  • Delivery timelines are estimates and may take longer during peak demand, bad weather or unforeseen circumstances.
  • Any applicable delivery charges will be shown or communicated before order confirmation.

Delivery availability

Delivery is available only in locations we can currently serve.

  • We deliver to selected serviceable locations in and around Bommasandra, Bengaluru.
  • All deliveries are subject to location and serviceability. An order can be accepted only if the delivery address falls within our service area.
  • Please confirm availability for your area before placing an order.
Please note: The 2-hour delivery estimate applies only to serviceable locations and is not a guaranteed delivery time for every address.

20-litre Bisleri jar return policy

Return terms for eligible jars supplied by NeerBeku.

60 daysReturn window from delivery
₹10Usage deduction per accepted jar
Bisleri onlyOriginal 20L jars supplied by NeerBeku
  • Only original 20-litre Bisleri jars supplied by NeerBeku are eligible under this return policy.
  • The jar must be returned within 60 days from the date of delivery.
  • If the jar is not returned within 60 days, it becomes the buyer's property and will no longer be eligible for return or refund.
  • A ₹10 usage and handling deduction per accepted jar will be applied when the jar is returned.
  • The applicable refundable amount will be calculated from the jar deposit or payment arrangement, after the deduction.
Brand restriction: Kinley, Bailley and other brands' jars are not eligible for return under this policy.
🔎

Jar inspection & acceptance

Every jar is checked before it is accepted for return.

  • Our delivery team will inspect each jar at the time of return.
  • Jars with fungus, contamination, cracks or other physical damage will not be accepted for return.
  • Only jars that pass the inspection will qualify for the applicable refund, after the ₹10 deduction.
  • Please keep the jar available and reasonably clean for inspection when arranging a return.
